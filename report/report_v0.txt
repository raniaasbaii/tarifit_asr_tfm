Defined the task: Tarifit ASR with output in IRCAM-based Latin script, mainly focused on Al Hoceima speech.
Built a metadata inventory of public YouTube and Instagram recordings from different content types.
Downloaded available audio and documented unavailable/private sources instead of deleting them from the inventory.
Standardized all usable audio to WAV, 16 kHz, mono.
Selected 21 recordings totaling 5 h 29 m 30 s.
Added three short female-speaker recordings to improve diversity in the training data.
Assigned speaker_group_id values and ensured recordings sharing a speaker stayed in the same dataset split.
Created a speaker-independent train/validation/test split.
Tested Whisper Small on REC038; it detected Arabic, produced unsuitable Arabic-script output, and gave poor word boundaries.
Replaced Whisper-based segmentation with Silero VAD.
Solved environment compatibility problems by using Python 3.12 and numpy<2.
Tuned VAD on REC038:
500 ms silence produced excessive fragmentation.
1000 ms silence produced better, more natural segments.
Approved segmentation configuration vad_v1:
minimum speech: 500 ms;
minimum silence: 1000 ms;
maximum segment: 15 s;
padding: 200 ms.
Segmented the full selected corpus into 2,223 segments, representing 5 h 03 m 31 s of detected speech.
Manually reviewed segments and used two statuses: included and rejected.
Final usable corpus:
2,175 included segments;
48 rejected segments;
4 h 59 m 19 s usable speech.
Final included segments by split:
train: 1,696;
validation: 289;
test: 190.
Kept rejected audio files for traceability rather than deleting them.
Decided that orthographic normalization will use IRCAM Latin as its foundation and define consistent rules for spontaneous-speech phenomena and borrowed words.

Designed a machine-assisted transcription pilot using 30 training segments selected from multiple speakers.
Used the same audio segments across candidate models to ensure a fair qualitative comparison.
Evaluated the candidates according to transcription accuracy, correction effort, script compatibility, omissions, hallucinations, and dialect substitutions.
Tested the following candidate models:
ayymen/stt_zgh_fastconformer_ctc_small;
facebook/mms-1b-all with the Kabyle adapter;
TutlaytAI/wav2vec2-berber;
UBC-NLP/Simba-S;
OpenAI Whisper.
Found that some systems produced output in unsuitable scripts or introduced substantial language and dialect substitutions.
Found that general multilingual models were not sufficiently reliable for direct Tarifit transcription assistance.
Found that TutlaytAI/wav2vec2-berber produced the most useful drafts among the tested candidates, with many correctly recognized words and output directly in an IRCAM-compatible Latin script.
Identified TutlaytAI/wav2vec2-berber as the provisional candidate for machine-assisted transcription.
